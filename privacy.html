PonyUp Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the PonyUp mobile application ("PonyUp," "we," "us") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By creating an account, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to create and operate your account:

  • Mobile phone number — used to create your account and to send you one-time login verification codes by SMS.
  • Display name — shown to other members of plans you join.

We also store the activity you create inside the app (plans, commitments, and wallet ledger entries) so the app can function.

How We Use Your Information

Your phone number is used to verify your identity at login and to operate your account. We use your information solely to provide the app's features.

SMS and Phone Number Sharing

We do not share your phone number, or your consent to receive text messages, with any third parties or affiliates for their own purposes. Your phone number is never sold. We do not use your phone number to send marketing or promotional messages. Text messages are limited to one-time verification codes that you request when logging in.

How Your Information Is Stored

Your information is stored in a secured cloud database and is accessible only as needed to operate the app. We retain your information for as long as your account is active.

Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information at any time by contacting us at the email below.
